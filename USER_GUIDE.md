# ORINOKO AppSaaS - User Guide

## Welcome to ORINOKO AppSaaS

ORINOKO AppSaaS is an AI-powered field service management platform designed to help local service businesses manage their entire operation from one intelligent platform.

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Go to the ORINOKO AppSaaS landing page
2. Click "Sign Up" button
3. Enter your email address
4. Create a strong password
5. Click "Create Account"
6. Verify your email address
7. Complete your profile information
8. Set up your company/organization
9. You're ready to go!

### First Steps

After creating your account, you'll be guided through:

1. **Organization Setup**
   - Company name
   - Logo and branding
   - Primary address
   - Contact information
   - Service areas

2. **Service Configuration**
   - Add your services
   - Set pricing
   - Define service categories
   - Set estimated durations

3. **Team Setup**
   - Add team members
   - Assign roles (Admin, Dispatcher, Technician)
   - Set permissions

4. **Initial Settings**
   - Currency and timezone
   - Language preference
   - Email settings
   - Default payment terms

## Main Features

### 1. Dashboard

Your central command center showing:

- **New Leads**: Incoming prospective customers
- **Pending Quotes**: Awaiting customer approval
- **Scheduled Jobs**: Upcoming appointments
- **In Progress**: Active work
- **Revenue Summary**: Today and this month
- **Available Technicians**: Ready for dispatch
- **Recent Activity**: Team actions and updates
- **Alerts**: Important notifications

**How to use:**
- Click any widget to drill down to that section
- Use date range selector to view different periods
- Set custom alerts in Settings

### 2. Leads Management (CRM)

Manage prospective customers through the sales pipeline.

#### Viewing Leads

**Kanban View** (recommended):
- Drag and drop leads between pipeline stages
- See lead count per stage
- Quick preview of lead info
- Filter by source, priority, assigned user

**List View**:
- Table format with sorting/filtering
- Bulk actions (assign, change status, delete)
- Advanced search
- Export to CSV

#### Creating a Lead

1. Click "+ New Lead" button
2. Fill in customer information:
   - Name and contact details
   - Service requested
   - Location/address
   - Lead source (where they came from)
   - Priority level
3. Add notes if needed
4. Assign to team member
5. Click "Create"

**Lead Details Page:**
- View all lead information
- Add/view notes and comments
- See communication history
- Check scheduled activities
- Convert to customer when ready

#### Pipeline Stages

1. **New Lead** - Just entered the system
2. **Contacted** - Initial conversation made
3. **Appointment Scheduled** - Meeting booked
4. **Estimate Sent** - Quote provided
5. **Estimate Approved** - Customer approved quote
6. **Job Scheduled** - Work scheduled
7. **In Progress** - Work being done
8. **Completed** - Job finished
9. **Payment Pending** - Awaiting payment
10. **Closed** - Won customer, job complete
11. **Lost** - Customer chose competitor

#### Tips
- Assign leads quickly to avoid delays
- Update status as conversations progress
- Use notes to track follow-up needed
- Convert quality leads to customers ASAP

### 3. Customers

Manage your customer database.

#### Customer Directory

- Search customers by name, phone, email
- Filter by customer type (residential, commercial)
- View total spent and job history
- See preferred contact method

#### Customer Details

View complete customer profile:
- Contact information
- Complete communication history
- All jobs/estimates/invoices
- Recent activity
- Notes and tags
- Links to documents

#### Adding Customers

1. Click "+ New Customer"
2. Enter contact information
3. (Optional) Link to a lead
4. Set customer type and preferences
5. Click "Create"

**Note**: Leads can be converted to customers, which auto-fills most information.

#### Managing Relationships

- Update contact information anytime
- Add notes about preferences
- Assign primary contact person
- Track communication preferences
- Monitor lifetime value

### 4. Calendar & Appointments

Interactive calendar for scheduling.

#### Viewing Calendar

**Views Available:**
- **Day**: Detailed hourly view
- **Week**: 7-day overview with technician lanes
- **Month**: Full month overview

**Filter By:**
- Technician
- Service type
- Status
- Customer

#### Creating Appointment

1. Click on desired date/time slot
2. Or click "+ New Appointment"
3. Fill appointment form:
   - Select customer
   - Choose service
   - Assign technician
   - Set date and time
   - Add notes
4. Click "Create"

#### Managing Appointments

- **Drag to Reschedule**: Click and drag appointment to new time
- **Edit**: Click appointment to open details, make changes
- **Confirm**: Mark as confirmed with customer
- **Remind**: System sends automatic reminders (24 hours before)
- **Cancel**: Remove appointment (notifies customer)

#### Technician Availability

- Each technician has their own schedule
- Set working hours in Technician settings
- Block out vacation/unavailable time
- System prevents double-booking

#### Tips
- Schedule appointments as soon as leads agree
- Set realistic travel time between jobs
- Group nearby appointments
- Use notes for special instructions

### 5. Technicians

Manage your field service team.

#### Technician Directory

- View all team members
- Check current status (available, on-call, busy)
- See average rating from customers
- Track total jobs completed
- Monitor performance metrics

#### Creating Technician Profile

1. Go to Technicians section
2. Click "+ New Technician"
3. Enter information:
   - Name and contact details
   - Photo (optional)
   - Specialties/services they perform
   - Certifications
   - Service area (radius)
   - Hourly rate
4. Click "Create"

#### Technician Details

View complete profile:
- Work history (recent jobs)
- Performance metrics (avg rating, avg duration)
- Customer feedback
- Specialties and certifications
- Availability schedule
- Earnings summary

#### Managing Technicians

- **Assign Jobs**: Drag jobs to technician on calendar
- **Set Availability**: Define working hours
- **Track Performance**: Monitor ratings and speed
- **View Earnings**: See total revenue generated

#### Tips
- Keep certifications current
- Route jobs efficiently to minimize travel
- Recognize top performers
- Address performance issues early

### 6. Services & Pricing

Configure what you offer and pricing.

#### Service Catalog

1. Go to Settings → Services
2. View all active services
3. Click service to edit details
4. Deactivate services no longer offered

#### Creating Service

1. Click "+ New Service"
2. Enter details:
   - Service name
   - Category
   - Description
   - Base price
   - Estimated duration (minutes)
3. Click "Create"

#### Pricing Strategy

- Set base prices for each service
- Allow markup for customer-specific needs
- Consider material costs vs labor
- Adjust prices seasonally
- Offer package discounts

#### Tips
- Keep service list current
- Review pricing quarterly
- Test pricing with leads
- Provide value, not just low price

### 7. Estimates & Quotes

Professional quotes for customers.

#### Creating Estimate

1. Go to Estimates section
2. Click "+ New Estimate"
3. Select customer
4. Add line items:
   - Choose service
   - Set quantity and unit price
   - Or add custom line item
5. Set:
   - Expiration date
   - Deposit required
   - Terms & conditions
   - Notes for customer
6. Review totals (including tax)
7. Click "Create"

#### Estimate Details

- View professional quote format
- See breakdown of all items
- Review total with tax and discount
- Add custom notes
- Attach photos or documents

#### Sending Estimate

1. Click "Send" button
2. Choose delivery method:
   - Email to customer
   - SMS (text)
   - Share link
3. Customer receives and can approve/reject
4. Track when opened

#### Approving/Converting

- Customer approves estimate
- Automatically creates Job Order
- Ready to assign technician and schedule
- Approved estimate is binding

#### Tips
- Send estimates quickly (within 24 hours)
- Set reasonable expiration dates (7-14 days)
- Include detailed descriptions
- Add photos of what needs fixing
- Proofread before sending

### 8. Jobs & Work Orders

Track work from start to finish.

#### Creating Job

Can be created from:
1. Approved estimate (recommended)
2. New job from scratch
3. Appointment

Fill in:
- Customer and location
- Services to be performed
- Assigned technician
- Scheduled date/time
- Estimated cost

#### Job Status Workflow

1. **Draft** - Being prepared
2. **Scheduled** - Technician assigned, time set
3. **Dispatched** - Sent to technician
4. **On the Way** - Technician traveling
5. **Arrived** - At customer location
6. **In Progress** - Work being done
7. **Completed** - Work finished
8. **Invoiced** - Invoice generated
9. **Paid** - Payment received

**Other Statuses:**
- Waiting for Parts - Paused, parts on order
- Cancelled - Job cancelled

#### During Job

Technicians can:
- Take before/after photos
- Add notes and observations
- Check off items from checklist
- Record materials used
- Request signature
- Note any issues

#### Completing Job

1. Technician marks "Complete"
2. Final cost calculated
3. Invoice automatically created
4. Customer can receive updates
5. Review and approve cost
6. Generate invoice

#### Job Details

View:
- Complete work history
- All photos taken
- Notes and observations
- Materials used
- Time spent
- Customer communication
- Invoice/payment status

#### Tips
- Schedule jobs with enough time
- Provide detailed customer notes
- Take photos for quality assurance
- Follow up promptly
- Get customer sign-off

### 9. Invoices & Payments

Billing and payment tracking.

#### Creating Invoice

Typically auto-generated from completed job:
1. Job marked complete
2. Invoice created automatically
3. Review invoice details
4. Send to customer
5. Customer pays

#### Manual Invoice

1. Go to Invoices
2. Click "+ New Invoice"
3. Select customer
4. Add line items
5. Set payment terms
6. Send to customer

#### Invoice Details

- Invoice number (auto-generated)
- Issue and due dates
- Itemized charges
- Tax calculation
- Payment instructions
- Notes for customer

#### Sending Invoice

- Email to customer
- Print to PDF
- SMS reminder
- Direct payment link (Stripe integration)

#### Tracking Payments

- See payment status (unpaid, partial, paid)
- Record payment when received
- Send payment reminders
- Track overdue invoices
- Run aging report

#### Payment Methods

- Cash
- Check
- Credit card (Stripe)
- Bank transfer
- Automatic payments (setup future)

#### Tips
- Send invoices immediately after job
- Set clear payment terms (Net 30, etc.)
- Send reminders at 10 and 20 days overdue
- Offer online payment option
- Follow up on overdue invoices

## Troubleshooting

### I can't log in
- Check email and password
- Use "Forgot Password" to reset
- Check if account is active
- Try incognito/private browser

### Calendar won't load
- Refresh the page (F5)
- Check internet connection
- Clear browser cache
- Try different browser

### Estimate won't send
- Verify customer email is correct
- Check your email configuration in settings
- Ensure internet connection
- Try sending again in 5 minutes

### Job won't change status
- Check your permissions
- Ensure required fields are filled
- Refresh page and try again
- Contact admin if issue persists

### Can't see another user's work
- Multi-tenant: You only see your organization's data
- Ensure user is in same organization
- Check user's role and permissions
- Ask admin if unsure
